# MackSims Privacy Policy (Beta)

**Effective date:** 2026-07-27  
**Controller:** MackSims  
**Contact:** feedback@macksims.com · csims@macksims.com  
**Site:** https://www.macksims.com/

This policy covers MackSims products currently offered as **external betas**, including FishCrew, ShutterBid, CurbCue, MotoCrew, CoachCore, Pastor’s Sermon Studio, and related marketing sites.

## 1. What we collect

Depending on the product and whether you sign in, we may process:

- **Account data** — name, email, auth provider identifiers (e.g. Google / GitHub OAuth)
- **Product content you enter** — drafts, ride notes, comparisons, check-ins, feedback
- **Device / usage data** — approximate region, app version, basic diagnostics
- **Beta requests** — contact info and product interest you submit
- **Partner outreach replies** — if you email us from a business address

Some beta surfaces still store data **only on your device** (`localStorage`). Cloud sync runs only when a product is configured with Supabase (or similar) and you sign in.

## 2. How we use data

- Provide and improve beta products
- Authenticate users and sync data you choose to save in the cloud
- Respond to feedback and partner inquiries
- Measure aggregate product usage (when analytics are enabled)
- Comply with law and protect security

We do **not** sell personal information.

## 3. Demo and simulated data

Several betas include **demo, mock, or estimate** data (for example simulated rides, rosters, or fare estimates). Demo content is not a live quote, booking, medical record, or official government feed. Do not enter real minors’ data, protected health information, or confidential third-party data into demo fields.

## 4. Sharing

We may share data with:

- **Infrastructure processors** (hosting, auth, email) under contract
- **Auth providers** you choose (Google, GitHub, etc.)
- **Authorities** when legally required

Partner outreach uses publicly listed **business** emails; see our outreach compliance notes for collection practices.

## 5. Retention

- Local-only data remains until you clear site data or uninstall
- Cloud account data is retained while your beta account is active
- You may request deletion by emailing feedback@macksims.com

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS) and access controls on cloud projects. Betas may still evolve; do not treat them as production systems for highly sensitive data.

## 7. Children

MackSims betas are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required). CoachCore demos must not be used to store real youth athlete personal data until parental-consent workflows ship.

## 8. International

Data may be processed in the United States or other regions where our providers operate. Bermuda and other users should contact us with localization questions.

## 9. Your choices

- Decline optional OAuth / cloud sync and use local-only modes where offered
- Request access, correction, or deletion: feedback@macksims.com
- Unsubscribe from partner email by replying STOP

## 10. Changes

We will update this page when production launches or material practices change. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy for beta use.
