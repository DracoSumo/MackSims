Privacy Policy (Beta)

Effective 2026-07-27 · MackSims · feedback@macksims.com

This policy covers MackSims beta products including CurbCue, FishCrew, ShutterBid, MotoCrew, CoachCore, and Pastor’s Sermon Studio. Full text also lives in the monorepo at legal/PRIVACY.md.

1. What we collect

Depending on the product and whether you sign in: account email / OAuth identifiers; content you enter (searches, comparisons, polls, feedback); device / usage basics; beta request fields. Some surfaces stay on-device (localStorage) until cloud sync is enabled.

2. How we use data

To run and improve betas, authenticate, sync data you choose to save, respond to feedback, and protect security. We do not sell personal information.

3. Demo and simulated data

CurbCue may show demo, mock, or tariff-based estimates — not live meter quotes or bookings. Do not enter other people’s personal data into crowd polls.

4. Sharing

Infrastructure processors, auth providers you choose, and authorities when required by law.

5. Retention & choices

Local data until you clear it; cloud data while your account is active. Request deletion at feedback@macksims.com. Decline OAuth to stay local-only where offered.

6. Children

Betas are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required).
