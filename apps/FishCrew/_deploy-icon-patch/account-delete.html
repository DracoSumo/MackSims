Account

Delete your FishCrew account.

You can remove this profile from the device in the app, then ask support to delete connected cloud data.

In the app

Open FishCrew → Settings → Delete account. That clears local profile, posts, and messages on this device. Or open the in-app flow at /account-delete.

Cloud / store deletion request

Email support@fishcrew.app from the same address you used to sign in. Ask for account deletion. We remove auth, profile rows, and uploaded media that are no longer needed for safety, fraud, or legal holds.
