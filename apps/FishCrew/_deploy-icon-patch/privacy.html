Privacy

FishCrew privacy policy.

FishCrew uses account, trip, feed, location, media, and marketplace information to run crew matching, safety, moderation, support, and app operations.

Data We Use

Profile details, posts, trip requests, crew chat, business inquiries, uploaded media, reports, approximate or permission-based location, device storage, and diagnostics needed to keep the app working.

How Data Is Used

Data powers trip discovery, private crew coordination, feed sharing, local partner inquiries, moderation, safety controls, support, and account features. Exact meetup locations stay private until crew approval.

User Controls

Users can edit profiles, report content, block profiles, delete local account data, and contact support for connected-data deletion when shared data is enabled.

Contact

Email support@fishcrew.app for privacy, deletion, safety, or account questions.
