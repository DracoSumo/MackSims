Privacy

FishCrew privacy policy.

FishCrew uses account, trip, feed, media, and marketplace information to run crew matching, safety, moderation, support, and app operations. Last updated: September 25, 2026.

Account information

Name, email, user id, role, home water, and profile details you provide. Auth is handled by Supabase when you create an account.

Photos and videos

Catch photos, profile media, and trip uploads you choose to add. Other users may see posts you make public. The Android app uses the system file picker and does not request camera, microphone, or contacts permission.

Location

You may type a water or area name. The current Play/TestFlight wrapper does not collect device GPS (no location permission). Optional in-browser GPS tools on the website may prompt only when you tap them. Meetup spots stay private until crew approval.

How data is used

Trip discovery, crew coordination, feed sharing, charter or shop inquiries, moderation, safety, support, and account features. We do not sell personal information.

Your controls

Edit your profile, report content, block profiles, and delete your account. In-app delete clears this device. Email support@fishcrew.app for cloud deletion. See also the store deletion page.

Contact

Email support@fishcrew.app for privacy, deletion, safety, or account questions. Support: /support.
