Privacy Policy (Beta)

Effective 2026-07-27 · MackSims · feedback@macksims.com

Covers MotoCrew and other MackSims betas. Canonical: legal/PRIVACY.md.

What we collect

Account / OAuth data when you sign in; ride drafts, joins, profile fields, emergency contacts you enter; basic device diagnostics. Local-only modes keep data on-device until sync is enabled.

How we use it

Operate the beta, sync when you opt in, improve the product, and respond to feedback. We do not sell personal information.

Safety note

Emergency contacts and checklists are tools — not a substitute for calling local emergency services. Do not rely on mocked map / pack-awareness surfaces as live tracking.

Your choices

Request deletion at feedback@macksims.com. Clear site data to wipe local storage.
