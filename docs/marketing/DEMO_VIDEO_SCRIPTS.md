# MackSims Demo Video Scripts

Record on a phone or tablet against the live Netlify / product URLs. Prefer screen recording + voiceover; optional face-to-camera openers for FishCrew / ShutterBid.

**House style**
- First 2 seconds: product name on screen
- Natural ambient sound under VO where possible (water, idle engines, night street)
- End card 2s: MackSims + Join the beta + URL
- No fake “live GPS” claims; if a surface is mocked, film the honest core loop only

---

## 0. Brand reel — MackSims (30–45s)

**Visual:** Quick cuts across FishCrew → ShutterBid → CurbCue → MotoCrew → CoachCore → Sermon Studio.

**VO:**
> Real-world communities don’t run on generic software.  
> MackSims builds one app per job — fishing crews, photo jobs, fare decisions, group rides, team accountability, sermon prep.  
> External betas are live. Join once. Pick your product.

**On-screen text:** MackSims · Software for real-world communities · Join the beta

---

## 1. FishCrew (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Captains and crews coordinate charters and days on the water.

**15s VO:**
> Running a charter on texts is chaos.  
> FishCrew keeps the crew, the trip, and the day in one place.  
> Join the MackSims beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Open home / trip list (3s)
2. Open a charter or crew screen (5s)
3. Show messaging or roster action (8s)
4. End card (3s)

**B-roll ideas:** dock lines, cooler lids, sunrise marina, captain checking phone on the gunwale.

---

## 2. ShutterBid (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Photographers get jobs, bids, and bookings without DM chaos.

**15s VO:**
> Jobs shouldn’t die in DMs.  
> ShutterBid is bids, bookings, and photographer profiles — one marketplace.  
> Join the MackSims beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Photographer profile (3s)
2. Open job / bid flow (8s)
3. Confirm booking moment (6s)
4. End card (3s)

**B-roll:** camera strap, venue walk-through, golden-hour portrait silhouette (no client faces without release).

---

## 3. CurbCue (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Riders compare options before they book; venues care about safer exits.

**15s VO:**
> Know where to ride before you book.  
> CurbCue compares options and shows pickup pressure — before prices jump.  
> MackSims beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Search a Hamilton / nightlife destination (4s)
2. Compare cards sort Best / Cheap / Fast (10s)
3. CrowdMeter or pickup tip (6s)
4. End card (3s)

**Partner cut (venues):** Same footage + VO swap:  
> Your guests leave through chaos. Partner with CurbCue so they leave with a plan.

**Do not film:** admin / government shells.

---

## 4. MotoCrew (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Hosts plan group rides; packs show up prepared.

**15s VO:**
> Plan the ride. Ride with the pack.  
> MotoCrew is meetup logistics, roster, and safety — built for riders.  
> Join the beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Tonight’s pack / ride card (4s)
2. Pack roster + checklist (10s)
3. Safety / create ride tease (6s)
4. End card (3s)

**Do not claim:** live GPS tracking if map is still placeholder — film planning + checklist.

---

## 5. CoachCore (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Coaches see who is locked in vs slipping.

**15s VO:**
> No more guessing who is locked in.  
> CoachCore puts film, workouts, and check-ins in one accountability view.  
> MackSims beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Dashboard accountability strip (5s)
2. Athlete needing a nudge (8s)
3. Assign / check-in action (7s)
4. End card (3s)

**Avoid:** medical claims, CrossFit trademark misuse, real minor athlete data.

---

## 6. Sermon Studio (15s / 30s)

**Job:** Pastors draft, series-plan, and export without losing the week.

**15s VO:**
> Draft the sermon. Plan the series. Export the notes.  
> Pastor’s Sermon Studio — focused prep for ministry staff.  
> Join the MackSims beta.

**30s beats:**
1. Outline editor (5s)
2. Scripture / worship tab (8s)
3. Library save or export (7s)
4. End card (3s)
