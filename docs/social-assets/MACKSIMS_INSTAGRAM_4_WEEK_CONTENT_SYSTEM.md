# MackSims Instagram: community-led app spotlight series

**Account:** `@macksims_` (Instagram Business)  
**Timezone:** Eastern Time (`America/New_York`; UTC-4 during this calendar)  
**Campaign window:** Tuesday, August 4–Thursday, August 27, 2026  
**Cadence:** exactly three feed posts per week, plus supporting Stories  
**Publishing state:** planning only; every item remains a draft until a human approves it

This replaces the earlier portfolio-heavy launch calendar with a softer, community-led series. One app gets the full week. Do not overwrite the original launch artwork under `instagram-launch/`. Create new production files under `instagram-app-weeks/`.

## Voice standard

- Lead with a familiar community moment or frustration, not a product catalog.
- Write to one person in that community. Prefer `you` / `your crew` over studio branding.
- Keep founder presence minimal unless a specific personal note is clearly useful.
- Prefer plain language over phrases such as “focused workflow,” “product portfolio,” or “drive qualified exploration.”
- Invite a real response: ask what feels difficult, what they currently do, or what would help.
- Keep honest beta labels and safety limitations, explained conversationally.
- Use no more than four relevant hashtags.
- Never invent a user quote, testimonial, customer count, or result.

## Timing hypothesis

- **Tuesday, 3:00 PM ET** — community moment / discovery
- **Wednesday, 6:00 PM ET** — deeper walkthrough
- **Thursday, 12:30 PM ET** — conversation / tester question

These are starting hypotheses. After Week 2, review Insights and adjust only the weakest slot by 60–90 minutes.

## Series structure

Each week follows the same arc:

1. **Community moment** — the everyday friction, told warmly.
2. **Walkthrough** — 2–3 real demo screens / steps from the current public beta.
3. **Conversation** — one specific question and a soft invite to try the demo.

App order: **FishCrew → ShutterBid → CurbCue → MotoCrew**.
