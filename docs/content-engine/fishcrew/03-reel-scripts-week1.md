# Week 1 reel scripts (10)

Post cadence: **1/day for 7 days**, then use extras as backups.  
Primary keyword: **`TIDE`** (one funnel until it works).  
Secondary later: `BAIT`, `CREW`.

Film style: face-to-camera + simple B-roll. Phone is enough.

---

## Reel 01 — Moving water first

**Hook (on screen):** “Wrong tide = empty cooler”
**Spoken:**  
Most beginners ask “what bait?” before they ask “is the water moving?” In Tampa Bay inshore, a weak slack tide kills the bite more often than the wrong color paddle tail. Fish current seams first — grass edges, mangrove points, dock corners — then pick bait.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE** — I’ll DM the free morning window checklist.
**B-roll:** tide chart screenshot, grass flat, mangrove point.
**Caption CTA:** Comment TIDE for the checklist.

---

## Reel 02 — Live shrimp default

**Hook:** “If you only buy one bait…”
**Spoken:**  
For West Florida weekend crews, live shrimp still covers snook, redfish, trout, and pier fish. Rig simple: circle hook, enough weight to hold bottom in current, fish the shadow line. Fancy boxes come later.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.
**B-roll:** bait shop board, shrimp in aerator, casting under dock.

---

## Reel 03 — Kayak go / no-go

**Hook:** “Don’t launch just because it’s sunny”
**Spoken:**  
Sunny + 15–20 mph wind on open Tampa Bay is a bad kayak day. Check wind and wave notes before trailer hitch. If it’s protected mangrove water and wind is under ~10, you’re in a better window.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.
**B-roll:** kayak at ramp, whitecaps, phone weather screen (FishCrew conditions if available).

---

## Reel 04 — Pier shadow line

**Hook:** “Stop casting into empty water”
**Spoken:**  
On piers and bridges, fish the shade and the current seam first. Baitfish stack on shadow edges; predators cruise that line. Work parallel to the structure before you bomb casts into open glare.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.
**B-roll:** pier pilings, sabiki/spoon flash, shadow line.

---

## Reel 05 — Leader mistakes

**Hook:** “Your leader is too light”
**Spoken:**  
Inshore toothy water and dock barnacles eat light leaders. Step up: braid main + 20–30 lb fluoro/mono leader for snook/reds around structure. You’ll lose fewer fish and less tackle.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.
**B-roll:** tying leader, frayed leader tip, fish lip release.

---

## Reel 06 — First-light topwater

**Hook:** “This 20 minutes matters most”
**Spoken:**  
First light topwater over grass edges isn’t magic — it’s timing. Low wind, moving water, and bait on the surface. Miss that window and you’re grinding plastics all morning.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.
**B-roll:** sunrise grass flat, walk-the-dog lure, blowup (stock ok if labeled).

---

## Reel 07 — Find a crew (product bridge)

**Hook:** “Fishing alone is why you cancel”
**Spoken:**  
Half of “I didn’t go” is no buddy, no boat seat, no plan. FishCrew is for posting open seats, pier meetups, and beginner mangrove runs without doxxing your secret pin in public. Public area first — private meetup after approval.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE** for the checklist — link to FishCrew is in bio.
**B-roll:** app trip card screenshot (demo ok), group at ramp, cooler.

---

## Reel 08 — After cold front

**Hook:** “Slow down after a front”
**Spoken:**  
Hard north wind and bluebird skies push fish deeper and slower. Downsize bait, fish slower retrieves, work deeper holes and channels. Forcing a summer pattern after a front is why coolers stay light.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.

---

## Reel 09 — Pack list 60 seconds

**Hook:** “Leave the junk at home”
**Spoken:**  
Weekend kit: one spinning combo, pliers, leader, circle hooks, shrimp or 3 soft plastics, sunscreen, water, license screenshot. Extra boxes slow you down and don’t get used.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**.

---

## Reel 10 — Respect the spot

**Hook:** “Share the bite, hide the pin”
**Spoken:**  
Post the pattern: tide stage, bait, wind. Keep exact GPS for approved crew only. That’s how local water stays fishable — and how FishCrew dock reports stay useful without burning spots.
**CTA:** Comment **TIDE**. App in bio.
