# FishCrew niche decision

## Why FishCrew first

1. **Content that people already watch** — bait, tides, “should I go?”, gear mistakes, species ID.
2. **Product is real enough** — live beta at `https://fishcrew.macksims.com/` with weather/tide planning, crew trips, dock reports.
3. **Instagram is already in the app** — Meta connect + caption handoff ports exist.
4. **Natural keyword magnets** — `BAIT`, `TIDE`, `PACK`, `CREW`, `SNOOK`.
5. **Monetization path** — tips → email/DM list → app signup → charter/shop marketplace later.

## Positioning

- Brand line: *Your water, your crew, your window.*
- Content job: help Florida anglers pick the right window and find crew faster.
- Offer job: turn viewers into FishCrew beta users (and later charters/shops).

## What we will *not* do

- Fake income claims (“M4 in 2 days”).
- Exact secret GPS spots in public posts.
- Medical/legal advice; always note regs + safety.

## Success metrics (first 30 days)

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Posts / week | 5–7 Reels |
| Comment→DM conversion | ≥ 20% of commenters reply in DM |
| Email/waitlist captures | 200+ |
| App signups attributed | Track with UTM `utm_source=ig&utm_campaign=content_engine` |
| Avg watch % (IG) | Aim > 40% on educational Reels |
