## Title

**FishCrew Morning Window Checklist**  
*Tampa Bay & West Central Florida — planning aid*

## Before you trailer or walk to the pier

- [ ] **Wind** under your craft limit (kayak: be conservative; open bay ≠ mangrove creek)
- [ ] **Waves / chop** acceptable for your boat or kayak
- [ ] **Tide is moving** (avoid planning the whole trip around dead slack)
- [ ] **Rain / storm odds** checked for the next 4–6 hours
- [ ] **Bait plan** ready (live shrimp *or* 2–3 plastics — not 40)
- [ ] **License + regs** screenshot on phone (size/slot/season)

## First-light priority (inshore)

1. Grass edges and points with **current**
2. Mangrove tips / pocket mouths on the **moving** stage
3. Dock / bridge **shadow lines**
4. Only then: open flat wandering casts

## Don’t launch if…

- Lightning or dark cores on radar toward your water
- Wind + exposure combo you wouldn’t kayak sober and rested
- You’re guessing the tide stage with no chart/app check

## Share the pattern, hide the pin

Post wind, tide stage, and bait. Keep exact GPS for approved crew only.
