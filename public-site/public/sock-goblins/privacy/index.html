Sock Goblins privacy policy

MackSims LLC · Last updated 20 October 2026

Sock Goblins is a game. We don't ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and you don't need an account to play.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (socks, crew, upgrades, settings and stats) is saved on your phone. We don't receive it. Deleting the app, or tapping Settings > Erase all progress, deletes it.

Ads

The free version can show optional video ads when you choose a reward, such as doubling your away earnings. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • The No Ads purchase removes all ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which items you own. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information. Read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Sock Goblins is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: simsc32@gmail.com.
