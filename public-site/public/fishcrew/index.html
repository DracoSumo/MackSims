FishCrew

Post a trip. Share an invite. Approve your crew. Private meetup and chat unlock only for people you let in.

External beta Anglers & fishing crews

What is it?

FishCrew is the MackSims fishing beta for real crew coordination. The core loop is simple: post a trip with a public area and a private meetup pin, share an invite link, approve requests, then unlock crew chat.

Who is it for?

Recreational anglers and small crews around Tampa Bay / Florida waters who are tired of planning launches across five group chats.

What problem does it solve?

Plans scatter across weather tabs, texts, and pins. FishCrew keeps discovery, invite, approval, and private meetup details in one place — with exact pins locked until you approve someone.

Current status

FishCrew is in external beta and is the primary MackSims product bet. Try the invite loop on the live web app and send feedback. Native TestFlight / Play closed testing uses the shared tester list — start at the beta testing hub.

Test now

Web beta: fishcrew.macksims.com. Try: post trip → Invite → open ?trip= link → request → approve → chat. Feedback: feedback@macksims.com.
